FAQ

Common questions

Which Microsoft 365 licenses do we need?

It depends on the solution. Many Power Apps and Teams solutions work with standard Microsoft 365 licensing, while premium connectors, Dataverse capacity and some Intune features need additional licenses. We confirm licensing during discovery, before any build begins.

Do you work in our tenant or yours?

We build in your Power Platform development environment and tenant so you own the result. Access is granted with least-privilege roles and removed when the engagement ends.

Can you take over an app someone else built?

Yes. We audit existing Power Apps, flows and Teams apps for reliability, security and licensing exposure, then stabilize, refactor or rebuild as needed.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. After launch we offer optional support and enhancement plans, or a clean hand-off to your internal team with training and documentation.

How are projects priced?

We offer fixed-scope projects for well-defined work and time-and-materials for exploratory or evolving efforts. Reach out for a scoped estimate.
